Questions

Good to know

Are we in the same room the whole time?

Yes. You are side by side in one private couples suite, each with your own therapist, for the full two hours. You can talk quietly or simply rest; the room is yours.

Does it have to be a romantic couple?

Not at all. The suite is just as welcoming for friends, a parent and adult child, or two people celebrating something. Anyone who wants the experience side by side is welcome.

How does draping work?

Exactly as it does for a single session. Each of you undresses to your comfort level in private and stays fully draped the entire time, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

Should we book ahead?

We recommend it. Two therapists and a private suite mean fewer slots, so a few days out gives you the best choice of times, especially on weekends and around holidays.
