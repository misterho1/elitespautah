Frequently asked

Quick answers.

If we missed something, call us. (801) 839-8880, daily 10am–10pm.

Does Elite Spa Utah accept same-day appointments?

Yes. Same-day appointments are available based on therapist availability. Call (801) 839-8880 or book online; same-day slots show up automatically if open.

Where are you located?

1136 S State Street, Salt Lake City, UT 84111, in downtown Salt Lake City.

What are your hours?

Mon–Sun, 10AM to 10PM. Same hours every day.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes. Two therapists, one private suite, side-by-side massage. Couples massage is $165 for 60 minutes, $245 for 90 minutes, and $325 for 120 minutes. Learn more →

Are your therapists licensed?

Every therapist at Elite Spa Utah is licensed by the State of Utah. Required, no exceptions.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are delivered instantly by email and never expire. Any amount from $25 to $500. Buy a gift card →

What should I wear or bring?

Just yourself. We provide robes, slippers, draping, and everything else. Wear or bring whatever's comfortable to and from.

Is the infrared sauna included with massage?

Infrared sauna is a 30-minute add-on for $30, layered onto any massage at booking. CBD is also $30; cupping and hot stones are $20 each. See all services →

How far in advance should I book?

Same-day usually works for solo services. For couples massage on weekends, book 3–7 days ahead.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we strongly recommend calling first; therapist availability changes hour to hour.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations and reschedules with 24 hours' notice are no-charge. Less than 24 hours, a 50% fee may apply.

Do you take insurance?

We don't bill insurance directly. We can provide an itemized receipt for HSA/FSA claims if needed.
